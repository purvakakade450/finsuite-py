Data Sources

Where the information shown in FinSuite comes from.

Yahoo Finance
Live stock quotes, daily price history (open, high, low, close, volume) and company search for NSE, BSE, US and other markets.
Used in: Stock Market, Watchlist & Alerts, ML Prediction, Portfolio Tracker, Currency & Compare.
Yahoo Finance currency pairs
Live exchange rates such as USD/INR, used to convert currencies and to add up foreign holdings in rupees.
Used in: Currency & Compare, Portfolio Tracker.
AMFI (Association of Mutual Funds in India)
The official daily NAV of every Indian mutual fund scheme, from AMFI's free public file. No API key.
Used in: Mutual Funds & SIP.
mfapi.in
A free public mirror of AMFI's historical NAVs, used for fund charts and SIP returns. No API key.
Used in: Mutual Funds & SIP.
Google News
Latest news headlines from the last 7 days for the selected company or the overall market.
Used in: Stock Market (with headline sentiment), ML Prediction.
Your own inputs
All calculators and planners work only on the numbers you enter. No outside data is used.
Used in: Ratios, Finance Report, Assistant, Loans, Credit and Risk, Investment Call, Company Performance, Portfolio Dashboard, Goals, Growth and Target, Portfolio and Tax, Income Tax, Loan EMI, Budget Planner, Retirement and FIRE.
Your account
If you create an account, your watchlist, portfolio holdings and price alerts are saved on the FinSuite server so they follow you to any device. Passwords are stored only as a salted hash.
Used in: Watchlist & Alerts, Portfolio Tracker.
Market prices can be delayed by a few minutes. FinSuite is an educational tool and not investment advice.

ML Next-Day Price Prediction

Trains Linear Regression and Random Forest (scikit-learn) on the stock's own daily history — moving averages (7/30 day), daily return and volatility — then forecasts the next trading day's close. Models are tested on the most recent 20% of days they never saw, and compared with the naive guess "tomorrow = today". Data: Yahoo Finance, no API key.

—
How to read this
News mood vs the forecast
MAE – average price error · RMSE – like MAE but punishes big misses · MAPE – average error as % of price. Lower is better. Educational only — short-term prices are hard to predict and this is not investment advice.

Income Tax: Old vs New Regime

India, FY 2025-26 (AY 2026-27). Enter your yearly income; FinSuite works out tax under both regimes and shows which saves more. Simplified: special-rate income such as capital gains is not included.
